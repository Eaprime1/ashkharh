# Yod

## STORY

Yod is the point of creation — the seed-letter, the smallest unit of divine utterance from which all form descends. In the Play, Yod manifests as the moment just before an entity commits to an action: the held breath, the poised hand, the tensed intention. It is simultaneously ending and beginning.

# Stone Tankard

## STORY

The Stone Tankard is the gathering hall — the first named place within The Play where paths cross without yet committing to direction. Its walls are old, worn smooth by the hands of entities who passed through without leaving a name. The air inside holds the faint pressure of unspoken intention.
